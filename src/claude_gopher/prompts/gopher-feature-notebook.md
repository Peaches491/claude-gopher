## Feature: engineering notebook

Keep a daily engineering notebook at `{{agent_dir}}/notebook/YYYY-MM-DD.md`: what the user worked
on, decisions and why, findings, and links, drawn from what they tell you and from agent replies.
It is a record of the work, not of routing (that is the journal). Write it as dated bullets under a
heading per project; the user may ask you to read it back or summarize a range.
